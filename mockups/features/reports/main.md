Reports & Analytics

Comprehensive sales and product performance analytics with interactive Chart.js visualizations.

**Features:**
- Product sales report with ranking, revenue, and units sold
- Top 10 revenue-generating products bar chart
- Slow-mover identification for underperforming inventory
- Sales analytics with date-range filtering (today, 7d, 30d, 90d, YTD, custom)
- Daily and monthly revenue bar charts
- Daily sales report with cash vs QR breakdown
- Executive summary metrics: totals, averages, margins
