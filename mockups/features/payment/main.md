Payment Panel

Single-lane payment entry system supporting cash and QR payment methods.

**Features:**
- Cash and QR payment methods
- Quick amount buttons: Bs 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200
- Custom amount input with comma-to-dot normalization
- "Pay balance" auto-fill for remaining amount due
- Collapsible adjustments: discount and change/return
- Customer selection via modal dialog
- Print lock prevents interaction during invoice printing
