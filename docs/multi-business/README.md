# Vendei Multi-Business Architecture Initiative

> Transform Vendei from a single-business POS into a generic multi-business selling platform.

## Objective

Evolve Vendei to support multiple business types (supermarket, chicken store, butcher, bakery, clothing, hardware) through **configuration and capabilities** rather than business-specific code.

## Principle

The same core commerce engine serves all business types. Business-specific behavior is controlled through:

- **Business Profile** — defines what the business is
- **Capabilities** — defines what the business can do
- **Catalog Templates** — defines what the business sells
- **Product Attributes** — defines how products vary
- **Selling Modes** — defines how products are sold
